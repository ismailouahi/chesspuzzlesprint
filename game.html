Chess Puzzle Sprint

Chess Puzzle Sprint is a fast tactical training app focused on one goal: making your over-the-board decisions faster and stronger through high-volume puzzle practice.

How it works

Open the app, start a sprint, and solve as many puzzles as you can in short bursts. The app presents positions across tactical themes and ratings, helping you identify patterns quickly under light pressure.

Who is it for?

It is built for ambitious improvers, club players, and serious learners who want practical daily training. The puzzle range supports everyone from beginner to advanced competitors.

Main features

  • Fast chess puzzle solving for focused sessions
  • Designed to strengthen practical chess intuition
  • Offline mode for uninterrupted training
  • Free to play
  • More than 200,000 puzzles
  • Difficulty from beginner to advanced
  • Clean, distraction-free interface
  • Available on Google Play

Why it helps improve chess intuition

Intuition is not guesswork—it is memory and pattern recognition built through repetition. Chess Puzzle Sprint compresses high-quality repetitions into short daily sessions so tactical motifs become familiar and actionable in real games.
